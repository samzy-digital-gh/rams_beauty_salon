FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book for a bridal appointment?

We recommend booking your bridal appointment at least 2–4 weeks in advance to secure your preferred date and time. For peak seasons (December, Easter, and holidays), 6–8 weeks is ideal. A trial session is available and highly recommended for all brides.

What products do you use for makeup?

We use only certified, skin-safe, and premium beauty brands. Our kit includes products suitable for all skin tones, including deeper melanin-rich complexions. All makeup tools are sanitized before every appointment.

Do you offer home or on-location services?

Yes! We offer on-location services for weddings, traditional ceremonies, and photoshoots. A travel fee may apply depending on your location. Please contact us via WhatsApp for pricing and availability.

Who is the Course Revamping masterclass designed for?

Our Course Revamping is designed for aspiring makeup artists, beauty enthusiasts, and professionals wanting to upgrade their skills. We tailor the curriculum to your current level and goals.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. This allows us to offer the slot to other clients. Late cancellations may be subject to a rebooking fee. Please contact us as early as possible.
