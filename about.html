Who We Are

Born from a Vision of
Empowerment

At Ramstouch, we don't just apply makeup — we craft confidence. Our journey began with a simple but powerful vision: to create a sanctuary where every woman feels like a masterpiece. Whether it's a high-fashion formal installation or a clean, natural bridal look, our work is defined by precision and a deep understanding of skin and style.

We believe every woman is a canvas of natural radiance, and our mission is to highlight your unique beauty with professional artistry that lasts. We use only the finest products and the latest techniques to ensure that your look is not only stunning but also healthy for your skin and hair.

From expert Hair Fixing and Course Revamping to our signature Luxury Pedicures, we provide a head-to-toe experience designed for the modern woman who demands excellence — located at Bintu Complex, Kwashieman, Accra.

Meet the Founder

Ruby Mensah

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Ruby Mensah is a visionary beauty artist with years of experience in luxury bridal glam and professional hair artistry. Her passion for empowering women through beauty led to the creation of Ramstouch Beauty & Makeup Lounge in the heart of Kwashieman, Accra.

Under her leadership, the lounge has become a trusted name for excellence, known for its impeccable standards, warm hospitality, and consistently stunning results. Ruby personally oversees every team member's training to ensure the Ramstouch standard is never compromised.

"I started Ramstouch because I believe every woman deserves to feel like a queen — not just on her wedding day, but every single day. Beauty is not about perfection; it's about celebrating who you already are."
