Our Story

The journey of elegance and artistry.

Master Artistry

At Ramstouch, we don't just apply makeup; we craft confidence. Our journey began with a simple vision: to create a sanctuary where every woman feels like a masterpiece. Whether it is a high-fashion formal installation or a clean, natural bridal look, our work is defined by precision and a deep understanding of skin and style.

We believe every woman is a canvas of natural radiance, and our mission is to highlight your unique beauty with professional artistry that lasts. We use only the finest products and the latest techniques to ensure that your look is not only stunning but also healthy for your skin and hair.

From expert Hair Fixing and Course Revamping to our signature Luxury Pedicures, we provide a head-to-toe experience designed for the modern woman who demands excellence. Located at Bintu Complex in Kwashieman, we invite you to experience the touch of perfection that has made us a leader in Accra’s beauty industry.

Ruby Mensah

Founder & CEO

Ruby Mensah is a visionary beauty artist with years of experience in luxury bridal glam and hair artistry. Her passion for empowering women through beauty led to the creation of Ramstouch Beauty & Makeup Lounge. Under her leadership, the lounge has become a trusted name for excellence, known for its impeccable standards and personalized care.
